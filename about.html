What This Is

Above the Hatch is a free, independent fly fishing resource for fly anglers fishing Watauga, Ashe, and Avery Counties in the North Carolina High Country. It was built by a local angler to consolidate the information that's usually scattered across multiple websites, reports, and field guides into one place.

There are three tools: the Hatch Matcher, which helps you identify what's likely hatching and what to tie on based on the current month; the Fishing Report, which synthesizes weekly intel from local guides and fishing sources; and the Fly Glossary, a visual reference for every pattern in the box.

This is a personal project — not affiliated with any fly shop, guide service, or commercial entity. It will always be free.

How the Hatch Matcher Works

The Hatch Matcher is built from four hatch charts cross-referenced and combined into a single dataset calibrated for High Country elevation (3,300–4,000 ft). At that elevation, hatches run 1–2 weeks later than lower-elevation WNC streams — a detail most regional charts don't account for.

Select a month and the matcher shows you what aquatic insects are likely active, suggested fly patterns with hook sizes, and timing notes for when and where to fish them. It also flags regulation context for the water types most likely to be productive that month.

The data is static — it reflects typical seasonal patterns, not real-time conditions. Always cross-reference with the Fishing Report and current local intel before heading out.

How the Fishing Report Works

The Fishing Report is updated automatically every Thursday night. An automated pipeline scrapes the latest reports from three sources, checks whether any of them have been updated since the last run, and — if something has changed — uses AI to synthesize the content into a structured report covering current conditions, top fly patterns, tactics, and notable waters.

The report is AI-synthesized, not hand-written. It reflects what the source reports actually say — fly names, hook sizes, water conditions, stocking notes, TVA pulse times — not generalized advice. Due South Outfitters is weighted as the primary source since their guides fish these specific waters weekly.

Because this is automated, always verify conditions locally before heading out. For the most current real-time intel, call Due South Outfitters directly.
