✓ Low water tip: Avoid large strike indicators in clear low flows — fish see them. Use a dry dropper rig instead: tie your nymph 12–18" off the bend of a buoyant dry fly (Elk Hair Caddis or Chubby Chernobyl work well). More stealthy, more effective, and you might get two takes at once.
