✓ Low water tip: Avoid large strike indicators in clear low flows — fish see them. Use a dry dropper rig instead: tie your nymph 12–18" off the bend of a buoyant dry fly. More stealthy, more effective. Go smaller and more natural on pressured water.
△ Reduced stocking 2026–2028: Setzer State Fish Hatchery is undergoing major renovation. Stocking on Delayed Harvest waters is reduced and may be intermittent. Check ncpaws.org before planning a trip around fresh stockings.
